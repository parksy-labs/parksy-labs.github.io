Privacy Policy for Stock Book

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Stock Book is an offline inventory and sales tracking app for small merchants. This policy explains, simply, how the app handles data.

No data is collected

Stock Book does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, or device information. There is no account creation, no login, and no internet connection required or used by the app.

Where your data lives

All information you enter — products, stock, purchases, sales, and reports — is stored only in a local database on your own device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, because the app has no network access to send it with.

No ads, no analytics, no third-party services

Stock Book contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and does not integrate with any third-party service that would receive your data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from your device's app settings, permanently deletes all data associated with it. We hold no copy anywhere, so there is nothing further for us to delete on our end.

Children's privacy

Stock Book is a business utility for shop owners and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
